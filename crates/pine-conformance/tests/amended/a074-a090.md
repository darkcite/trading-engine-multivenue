# AMEND-06 — impl-06 §4.2.1, the A090 / A074 examples in §4.2.0 template form

Lead ruling 2026-10-17: §4.2.0's emission template governs; §4.2.1's A090/A074 examples are
illustrative and are replaced by the blocks below. Pinned as `pin:06-template-governs-examples`.

Replace the two ```pine blocks after the v6-bool table in §4.2.1 (the ones beginning
`// A090 · b[1] on the first bar` and `// A074 · the same in v5`) with these. Each block is the
generator's exact output except that the 13-line SYN-1 prelude (§4.1.1) is elided as the single
line `<SYN-1 prelude, §4.1.1>`. The test `group_a_amended_examples_are_byte_identical` compares
them (T5's "A074/A090 byte-identical" acceptance).

Differences from the old examples: the SPDX pair is on lines 1–2; `//@version` comes before
`indicator(…)`; the comment line comes after `indicator(…)` and reads
`<ID> · <title> · K10 × bool · dialects <d> · twin <ID> · pin:W13-bool-na · generated by pine-conf
gen; do not edit` (title = the kernel's `tests` column, `pin:06-a-titles`). The prelude precedes the
body.

# AMEND-06 (more) — impl-06 §4.2.2 B011, §4.2.3 C012, §4.2.4 D005, §4.2.5 E001 in §4.2.0 template form

Lead ruling 2026-10-17: §4.2.0's emission template governs the catalogue's examples
(`pin:06-template-governs-examples`, as in `AMEND-06-a074-a090.md`). Replace the ```pine example
blocks that open `// B011 ·` (§4.2.2), `// C012 ·` (§4.2.3), `// D005 ·` (§4.2.4) and `// E001 ·`
(§4.2.5) with the blocks below, in that order. Each block is the generator's exact output except that
the SYN-1 prelude (§4.1.1, 13 lines) is elided as `<SYN-1 prelude, §4.1.1>` and the SYN-T prelude
(§4.1.3, 13 lines with `FROM_MS` / `STOP_MS` as literals) as `<SYN-T prelude, §4.1.3>`. The test
`group_e_amended_examples_are_byte_identical` (tests/unit_gen_e.rs) compares them.

Differences from the old examples:

- all four: the SPDX pair on lines 1–2, `//@version` before the declaration, and the comment line after
  the declaration in the form `<ID> · <title> · <block> · dialects <d>[ · twin <ID>][ · <pin>…] ·
  generated by pine-conf gen; do not edit`; the prelude precedes the body (B011, C012 had none shown).
- B011: the comment also names `pin:04-ta-reference` (§4.9.7 assigns it to B001–B076).
- C012: the function-local `var` is suffixed too, `acc_C012` (§4.2.0: every identifier a fixture
  declares ends in `_<ID>`).
- D005: the title reads `240 close, gaps on, lookahead off` (the D-matrix row text), and the
  `request.security` call is on one line (`pin:06-d-no-wrap`: §4.2.0 permits wrapping inside parentheses
  but never requires it).
- E001: the comment's `(captured in v6 and v5)` aside is dropped (the `.fix` `captured_in` field
  records it after capture); the header comment follows the five-line `strategy(…)` declaration.
